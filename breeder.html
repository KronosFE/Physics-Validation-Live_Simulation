Read the badges honestly HYPERION is sized to the requirement, not to a physics optimum: the duty badge trips when net tritium leaves the 1.87–4.0 kg/yr band, and the closure badge needs on-duty and βN below the NSTX no-wall 4.2 and peak coil field within the demonstrated 20.1 T.
