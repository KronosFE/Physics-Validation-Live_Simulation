HYPERION · DESIGN CHOICE

Why negative triangularity

Most tokamaks point the plasma's D inward (δ > 0). Hyperion flips it — the D points outward (δ = −0.30). It trades a familiar shape for a calmer, reactor-friendlier edge.

Conventional +δHyperion −δ
Edge instabilities (ELMs)ELMysuppressed
Divertor heat pulsestransientsteadier
Core confinementgoodcomparable
Reactor robustnessbaselineedge

The tritium fuel cycle

Every D–T fusion fires a 14.1 MeV neutron into the blanket, where lithium breeds fresh tritium. Bred > burned (TBR 1.42) — the plant makes its own fuel, with a surplus.
