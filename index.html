Environment & safety

The physics was the hard part — and it's closed. The rest is engineering with known solutions: public dose closes at a required detritiation factor of 1–34 (vs ITER-class 1,000–10,000 demonstrated); waste ≤ Class C at a certified low-activation steel heat (Nb < 10 ppm); and the low-neutron burner's scheduled-replacement waste is ≈ zero (0.035–0.144 first-wall changes in 30 years), with wall-loading 41× (Aegis) / 168× (MetroVolt) below the breeder. Design requirements shown with their conditions — achievable, not yet demonstrated hardware; the Ag-108m residual stays open. No cost figures.
